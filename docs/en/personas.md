# Personas

## Chronic latecomer

- **Situation:** A university student with an irregular schedule of classes and appointments.
- **Goal:** *(inferred, not directly stated — flag for approval)* Stop being late, and stop being seen by friends as someone who doesn't keep time.
- **Frustration:** Frequently arrives late; feels anxious during every commute wondering if he'll make it; regularly teased by friends for it.
- **Current coping:** Mentally calculates the minutes remaining before each appointment, but habitually leaves zero buffer.

*"I'll just work it out when I leave — it's always fine." (illustrative paraphrase, not something actually said)*

## Anxious over-preparer

- **Situation:** A university student with the same kind of irregular schedule as the Chronic latecomer, but is never late.
- **Goal:** *(inferred, not directly stated — flag for approval)* Stop the nightly checking and recalculating, without becoming late.
- **Frustration:** The nightly vigilance has built up fatigue; feels anxious every time that she's forgotten something.
- **Current coping:** Every night, checks the next day's schedule and works backward from each appointment's start time to set minute-precise alarms for waking up and getting ready — also accounting for prep time, which varies by appointment (full makeup vs. casual).

*"I already checked three times — but what if I still missed something?" (illustrative paraphrase, not something actually said)*

## Transfer racer

- **Situation:** An office worker in their late 20s to early 30s who can't yet afford to live near the office, so commutes a long distance.
- **Goal:** Save every minute of the commute possible, to protect personal time on the other end for exercise, reading, and self-development.
- **Frustration:** Repeatedly watches the connecting Line 7 train leave right as they reach the platform during the transfer at Nonhyeon (Sinbundang line) or Gangnam-gu Office (Bundang line).
- **Current coping:** Manually checks/searches for the next train's arrival time each time, since without knowing it they can't tell whether sprinting the transfer is worth it.

*"I watched it pull away right as I got there — again." (illustrative paraphrase, not something actually said)*
